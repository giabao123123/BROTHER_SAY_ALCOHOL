04 / FORMULA CALCULATOR

SCORING SYSTEM

[During the show, contestants go through 3 dynamic singing rounds to showcase their vocal power, charisma, and emotional connection with the crowd. The scoring is very precise: each judge gives a score out of 10. The sum of the 3 judges' scores is then multiplied by 30. After that, the voting points from the 100 audience members—who can contribute up to 10 points each—are added to calculate the final score. The participant with the highest total score after 3 rounds is crowned the episode winner!]

[ But what happens if there is a tie? That is when the show gets super thrilling with the "Battle Aura" challenge! In this show, "aura" represents a artist's irresistible stage presence, charisma, and prominent style on stage—or stage presence, charisma, and prominent style on stage (AURA). In this battle, one singer performs two lines of a song, and the opponent must immediately sing a new song starting with the very last word of the previous lyric. They take turns in this fast-paced word-chain singing duel. Afterward, the 100 audience members vote either “Aura” or “No Aura”, and the one with more “Aura” votes takes the victory! ]
